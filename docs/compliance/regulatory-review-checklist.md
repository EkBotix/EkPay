# Regulatory review checklist

DRAFT / NOT LEGAL ADVICE — incomplete, requires qualified advice.

Confirm applicable Bangladesh laws/regulations and current circulars with counsel and official sources. Obtain written scope/classification opinion covering verification software, transaction-data access, merchant automation, account ownership, outsourced services and possible PSO/PSP obligations. Evaluate licences/approvals, reporting, AML/KYC responsibilities, data protection, consumer safeguards, provider permission and marketing wording. Record reviewer, date, evidence, approval conditions and renewal triggers; do not invent a signoff.

Review current Bangladesh Bank warnings before launch. This repository is not a legal/regulatory exemption analysis. Public reference platforms are design research only and their positioning is not evidence of compliance.

Non-custodial or software-only architecture does not itself establish regulatory exemption. Regulatory classification must be confirmed against applicable Bangladesh law, Bangladesh Bank rules/guidance, and qualified regulatory/legal advice before commercial launch.
