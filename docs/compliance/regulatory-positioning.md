# Regulatory positioning

DRAFT / NOT LEGAL ADVICE — Development / pre-launch.

Public positioning: Transaction Verification & Merchant Automation Software. No claim of Bangladesh Bank approval, licensing, compliance certification or exemption. No public payment service is enabled.

Non-custodial or software-only architecture does not itself establish regulatory exemption. Regulatory classification must be confirmed against applicable Bangladesh law, Bangladesh Bank rules/guidance, and qualified regulatory/legal advice before commercial launch.

Bangladesh Bank publishes payment-system licensing/oversight information [here](https://www.bb.org.bd/en/index.php/financialsystems/paysystems) and circulars [here](https://www.bb.org.bd/en/index.php/mediaroom/circular). These are research starting points, not a current classification opinion; some explanatory pages may lag legislation. Review applicable 2024 Act, current regulations, MFS/provider rules, subsequent circulars and official warnings with counsel before commercial operations. This implementation does not determine whether a licence/approval is required.

No live merchant signup campaign, provider connections, real SMS, authoritative verification, merchant webhook sends or BD21topup integration is authorized by Phase 3. Logical live test data is not commercial activation.
